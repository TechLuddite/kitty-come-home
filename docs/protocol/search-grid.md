# The search grid

**Purpose:** make sure you have actually searched a place, rather than remembering that you
walked past it.

After two days of panic, every garden looks like one you have already checked. People re-search
the easy, well-lit, accessible half of the radius repeatedly and never once look inside the
culvert. A written grid fixes this, and it is the piece the app is eventually meant to replace.

## Setting the radius

Work in rings from the **point of escape** — the specific door or window, not the middle of your
house.

| Ring | Radius | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Your own property | Search first, search completely, search again |
| 1 | ~50 m — roughly the adjacent five houses in each direction | **Highest.** The median find is 39 m |
| 2 | ~150 m — the 75th percentile for indoor-only cats | High |
| 3 | ~500 m — 75% of all cats are found inside this | Moderate; only after 1 and 2 are properly done |

Beyond ring 3 is rarely productive for an indoor-only cat and is usually a sign the inner rings
were searched too quickly.

**Resist the urge to jump outward.** As the days pass the instinct is to widen. The evidence says
the opposite: search the same close ground more carefully, at a better hour, with a better torch.

## What counts as a place

A grid square is not "the Hendersons' garden." It is each individual hiding place, because that
is what you either did or did not put a torch into.

For every property in the ring, the checklist is:

- [ ] Under the deck or porch — every access gap
- [ ] Crawlspace, and its vents
- [ ] Under the front and back steps
- [ ] Shed — inside, and underneath
- [ ] Garage — inside, and behind everything in it
- [ ] Under parked cars, and **inside the wheel arches and up on the engine block**
- [ ] Woodpile, and inside the gaps
- [ ] Under and inside garden furniture, barbecue covers, tarpaulins
- [ ] Bins, and behind the bin store
- [ ] Dense shrubs and hedge bases — from underneath, not from standing height
- [ ] Drains, culverts, storm channels
- [ ] Under raised outbuildings and playhouses
- [ ] Greenhouse, potting shed, compost bin
- [ ] Gaps under fences and gates
- [ ] Boats, trailers, caravans, campers
- [ ] Any building under construction or renovation

Cats choose the space that feels **enclosed on all sides and has one exit**. They go low, and
they go tight. If you can imagine getting into it, it is too big.

## Recording a search

For each place, record: the date, the time, whether it was day or night, and one of three
outcomes.

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Searched** | Torch went in. You saw the whole space. Nothing there. |
| **Partial** | You looked but could not see all of it. **Treat as unsearched.** Come back. |
| **Blocked** | You could not access it — locked shed, no answer at the door. **These are your highest-value targets.** |

The blocked list matters more than the searched list. The 11% of cats found inside someone
else's building were, until someone opened the door, sitting on a blocked line in somebody's grid.

## Re-searching

A "searched" square does not stay searched. The cat moves, usually at night, usually after the
first day or two of lying still.

- Re-search ring 1 **every night**.
- Re-search a place at a **different hour** than last time. A daytime search and a 2am search of
  the same shed are two different searches.
- After a credible sighting, re-search everything within ~50 m of the sighting immediately,
  regardless of when you last did it.

## Paper version

Until the app exists, a table in a notebook is entirely sufficient, and it is what the app will
be copying:

```
DATE  TIME  ADDRESS/PLACE                  WHAT              RESULT    NOTE
8/20  0130  14 Elm - under deck            deck              searched
8/20  0140  14 Elm - shed                  shed              BLOCKED   locked, ask owner
8/20  0155  16 Elm - crawlspace vent       crawlspace        partial   need better torch
8/20  0210  16 Elm - under blue pickup     vehicle           searched
```

Two columns do the real work: **BLOCKED**, which is your to-do list, and **partial**, which is
your list of lies you told yourself at 2am.
