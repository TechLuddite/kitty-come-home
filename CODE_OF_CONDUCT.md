# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be kind. People arrive here upset.

## Why this matters more than usual

Many of the people who open an issue in this repository will be in the middle of searching for
their own cat, or will have finished searching without finding it. Some will describe that in
detail. Some will be angry, or will ask questions that are already answered in the documentation.

Answer them anyway, and answer them gently. The cost of being short with somebody in that position
is much higher than the cost of repeating yourself.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good intent, especially when someone is blunt.
- Be patient with questions that are already answered elsewhere. Link the answer, do not point at
  it.
- Give credit for field experience. Someone who has trapped fifty cats knows things this
  repository does not, and their contribution is worth more than a tidy pull request.
- Disagree about the substance, not about the person. This project changes its mind when evidence
  says to.
- Respect people's privacy, including the privacy of neighbours and passers-by who appear in
  camera footage and never agreed to any of this.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Dismissing or mocking somebody's distress about a missing animal.
- Publishing anyone's private information — addresses, contact details, location data — including
  from screenshots or camera footage shared in an issue.
- Using this project to promote a paid service to people in the middle of a search.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately to the repository owner if that is not
appropriate. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break these rules.
Enforcement will err toward warning and explanation first, except for harassment and doxxing,
where it will not.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to anyone representing
the project elsewhere.
